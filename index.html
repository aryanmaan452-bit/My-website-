Good to know

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before you press play.

Redline is a concept streaming experience for discovering films, series, documentaries, and stories worth sharing. This demo page is a front-end design and does not provide actual streaming.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. A real product could support browsers, smart TVs, phones, and tablets after its streaming service is connected.

Subscription management is not connected in this demo. In a production service, account settings would explain plan changes, billing, and cancellation clearly.

The design includes a dedicated kids-profile feature. A live service should pair that feature with parental controls and an age-appropriate content catalogue.

Choose “Start watching” to reach the sign-up section. Connect that destination to your real registration or login flow when you integrate a backend.
